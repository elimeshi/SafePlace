**SourceLabel** — the colored provenance tag under a poster's name; the eye learns to read post origin by color (feed-spec §2.3.4–2.3.5). Soft fill, ~20px tall.

```jsx
<SourceLabel kind="group" name="חסדי שכונה" color="var(--group-sage)" icon="users" />
<SourceLabel kind="public" />
<SourceLabel kind="suggested" />
```
