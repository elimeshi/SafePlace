# 0000 — ADR Template

Copy this file to `NNNN-short-title.md` (next sequential number) when making
a non-trivial, hard-to-reverse technical decision.

## Status

`Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN`

## Context

What problem are we solving? What constraints (from the product spec, legal
tracker, or prior ADRs) bear on this decision?

## Decision

What did we decide, stated as a single clear sentence if possible.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it was not chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result. What follow-up work this creates
(e.g., an open question to add to `open-questions-backlog.md`, or a legal
item to add to `docs/legal/legal-tracker.md`).
